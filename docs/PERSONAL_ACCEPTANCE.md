# Genuine wallet and learning acceptance

This is a prepared checklist, not a completed test. Production financial access stays gated until the balance consistency contract and genuine acceptance are resolved. The builder reported a malicious-site warning in Slush PC extension **26.21.6 (e2be7f2)**. Stop wallet sign-in until official review resolves it; do not bypass the warning. A UI/API sign-in pause is prepared locally and must be separately verified after deployment. See `SLUSH_REVIEW_REQUEST.md`. Do not sign transactions, fund wallets or transfer tokens for this exercise.

1. Choose the public Sui Mainnet address controlled by the installed wallet you will use. Share only that public address, never a key, seed phrase, signature or delegate secret. Supported identity verification currently enables Ed25519, Secp256k1, Secp256r1 and Passkey personal-message signatures; other schemes fail explicitly rather than invoking a public RPC fallback.
2. Inspect the sign-in message: app origin, Mainnet, wallet, nonce, issue/expiry times and identity-only wording. Sign with the wallet UI; the app must never request gas, payment approval or transaction execution. Check the displayed account and sign out. A wrong/changed wallet must clear personal state and require sign-in again.
3. After the release gate permits the test, save an explicit planning end date, reserve and an ordinary short bill alias. Open a fresh browser session, sign in again and verify persisted values. Expected income must not increase available USDC.
4. For a real learning story, identify existing eligible historical payments. Three distinct supported, classified transfers to the same exact destination/token/category with local gaps of 6–8 days and amounts within 5% of median are required for a weekly suggestion. Do not create transfers or use the unrelated public reference wallet as if it were yours.
   If the selected wallet has no qualifying payment history, use an actual manually confirmed recurring commitment instead. Its envelope records `origin=manual` and must never be described as a history-derived suggestion. The acceptance wallet inspected during this build has zero USDC and no eligible simple native-USDC movements in the checked bounds; no funding is requested.
5. Prepare the exact short non-identifying explanation/rule/recurrence event payloads, with opaque ledger source IDs. Review them in a separate document before requesting permission for any additional Walrus writes. The earlier approval covered exactly 24 synthetic seed events and does not authorize these personal events.
6. After explicit approval, confirm the proposed recurrence. Capture queued, accepted, stored and matching-recall states; only the final confirmed state may change the estimate. Record the actual event, job and blob IDs and UTC observation. Preserve identical raw facts, explicit plan and horizon for the comparison.
7. Return in a later actual session, sign in, refresh signed recall and change the confirmed recurrence amount or deactivate it. Review/authorize that exact replacement event before submission if not already covered by the prior concrete approval. Verify the new revision supersedes the old while both remain inspectable. Record real dates; do not simulate weeks of use.
8. Record redacted screenshots and before/after numeric results, sources, active revisions and blob IDs in LEARNING_LOG.md. Inspect the export for correct user scoping. Do not publish raw personal exports or notes.

The public demo and synthetic replays remain useful for product review but cannot satisfy G13. No personal acceptance or learning write is claimed by this document.
